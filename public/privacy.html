Privacy notice

This independent STL raffle stores the participant name, contact number, agent username, selected supervisor name, and proof references in private raffle storage. Local development uses a data directory; production stores full entry details and screenshot files in private Supabase Storage buckets. Database tables retain only a ticket code, a protected contact hash, entry status, timestamps, and draw metadata.

The organizer uses this information to administer entries, review proof, contact winners, and handle prize claims. The admin dashboard and screenshot files are protected by the organizer passcode. The app does not request social media passwords, authentication codes, or account profile data.

The screenshots and entry details should be retained only as long as needed for the raffle and prize claims. Contact the organizer to request access to or deletion of your entry.

This raffle is organized independently by STL. PCSO and the linked pages are not sponsors or endorsers of this raffle.
